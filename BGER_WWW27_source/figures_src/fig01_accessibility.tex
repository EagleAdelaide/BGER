\Description{An original synthetic bottle and matching geometric logo template
illustrate whole-image product-to-template retrieval. The feature-grid icon
is schematic, not an evaluated model output. The product is encoded by a
backbone into spatial features H. Supervision placements are alternatives
in separately trained models. CE, Aux-BCE and CAM-BCE pool H directly;
BGER computes gate M and multiplies both H and M before pooling.
The pooled embedding branches to a training-only identity classifier and
to the pairwise cosine score. CE has no box loss. The template uses the same
selected-arm encoder and pooling. Separate off-path auxiliary-gate and
class-activation-proxy branches receive box BCE without feeding retrieval.
Both Grad-CAM hooks, at spatial H and BGER F before pooling, explain the
deployed pairwise score s(q,d), holding the template embedding z_d fixed,
not training-class logits. A subordinate ABN inset distinguishes attention-branch
class logits with CE from spatial attention logits a with box BCE. A equals
sigmoid(a), and (1+A) times stage-3 features G feeds perception stage 4 and
pooling. The bottle's dashed box is an annotation overlay, not a test-time
input or crop. Retrieval uses whole images without box or class-label inputs.}
